Defense Minutes

Panelist Comments/Suggestions/Recommendations are automatically loaded from their evaluation. Fill in Page No. and Action/s Taken for each item.

How does Defense Minutes work?

  • When your verdict is Passed with Revisions or Conditional Passed, each panelist's Comments/Suggestions/Recommendations from Outline, Pre-Final, or Final Defense are automatically added to your Defense Minutes under that panelist's name. (Comments for Passed, For Formal Defense, or Failed are sent via Notifications.)
  • Click View → Edit Text to fill in the Page No. and Action/s Taken for each panelist item (students cannot add entries or edit panelist comments), then click Save Minutes.
  • Once saved, click Send to forward your Defense Minutes to your Adviser for checking first, and then to the Panelists who graded your defense.
  • Both your Adviser and Panelists will review and affix their e-signatures to complete the document.
  • DEFENSE MINUTES SHOULD BE DONE PHYSICALLY.
